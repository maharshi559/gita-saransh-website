Waitlist privacy notice

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we keep only what you type into the form:

  • Your email address
  • Your WhatsApp number, if you choose to give it, and whether you agreed to WhatsApp updates
  • The language you want to learn in, and who the app is for
  • How you found us (for example, a link a friend shared), if the link says so

We don’t use advertising trackers on this site.

What we use it for

Only to tell you when Gita Saransh launches and to give you your founding member perk. We send WhatsApp messages only if you ticked the box to allow them.

Who can see it

Only the Gita Saransh team. Your details are stored with our database provider, Supabase. We never sell or share them with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Until the launch and founding-member offer are over, or until you ask us to remove it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email with one tap, reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, or write to or contact us to see, change or delete your details.

The app itself

The Gita Saransh app has its own privacy policy, which will be linked here at launch.
