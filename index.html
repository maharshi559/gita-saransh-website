Ask Krishna

Ask him anything. He answers at his age.

Worried about exams? Feeling angry? Krishna gives real advice and quotes the shloka behind it. As a toddler he giggles about butter pots. By Chapter 18 he speaks as he did to Arjuna. The wisdom stays the same.

  • Every answer quotes a real verse, checked, never made up
  • Gentle and safe for children, answered in your language

Questions families ask

Is it free?

Chapter 1 is free for everyone. After that, a small subscription unlocks all 18 chapters, with a free trial to start. Founding members get a special launch gift.

Which languages can I learn in?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Marathi, Bengali and Gujarati. Shlokas are always in Sanskrit; the meanings, stories and Krishna’s answers are in your language.

Do I need to know Sanskrit?

Not at all. You listen first while each word lights up, then say it back. The meaning comes right after, in your language.

Who is it for?

Children learning with a parent, adults starting the Gita for the first time, and grandparents who want to recite it daily. Everyone in a family can have their own Krishna.

When does it launch?

We’re launching soon on iPhone and Android. Founding members hear first.

Is Ask Krishna safe for kids?

Answers are gentle, family friendly and always tied to a real verse. If a question sounds like someone needs real help, the app steps out of character and points them to a trusted adult or helpline.

What do you do with my email and number?

Only tell you about the launch and your founding perk. You can unsubscribe with one tap. We never sell or share your details. Read the privacy notice.
